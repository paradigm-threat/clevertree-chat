A new way to debate.

A page can hold a standing debate. People add an original post to an argument. They do not reply down a thread. The place a point is answered is the other argument, and that argument has its own short address.

Don’t get lost in the weeds.

A topic and a one-line argument make the address. Sentences that make the same argument share a key. A different reason stays beside it, so someone watching can see the debate.

One post can sit in more than one key when those keys already exist. “These cameras are illegal mass surveillance.” is listed under invasion-of-privacy and illegal, and it stays one comment. A new key opens only when a post does not fit. “but it's illegal!” spills into illegal. The Patriot Act is cited as a suggestion. It is not opened as a directory.

Make your opinion finally heard.

A post joins the one-liner it actually makes. When it is a different argument, it opens a new one. The page does not pick a winner.

A post can belong to more than one argument, or to none. A new argument appears when a point spills out of the ones already there.

Clevertree Chat, called Cleverchat for short. The name can change. The debates stay.
